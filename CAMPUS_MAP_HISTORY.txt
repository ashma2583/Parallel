CAMPUS MAP PROJECT — CONVERSATION AND CHANGE HISTORY
====================================================

Purpose
-------
This file is a readable summary of the project requests and the related work
recorded in this conversation, from the initial "how do I activate the program"
question through the latest map-theme and marker requests. It is a change
history, not a verbatim transcript. It describes the implemented map-preview
work and notes where map content is illustrative rather than verified.

Conversation and implementation timeline
-----------------------------------------

1. Starting and activating the project
   The conversation began with a general question about how to start the
   program. The user tried `spacetime publish` and `spacetime start` from
   `backend/spacetimedb` and got `spacetime: command not found`. Earlier startup
   troubleshooting also covered missing SpacetimeDB server/dependency setup.
   This was setup guidance; it did not change the map feature code.

2. University of Michigan campus map
   The map work focused first on the University of Michigan and Ann Arbor:
   real-world map context, detailed buildings, movement, dormitories, and
   Michigan Stadium. A building hover-highlight experiment was removed after
   the user reported that it remained buggy. The later campus previews remain
   previews; selecting another school does not change the U-M simulation.

3. Campus map usability and power markers
   The user asked for map movement and zoom to feel less laggy, requested
   electricity-themed markers, and asked that forms show blue dots rather than
   names. Bus and route overlays were adjusted so they remain projected while
   the map moves, rather than disappearing during navigation. Bus animation
   progress is kept stable across map redraws and pauses while the user pans or
   zooms. Power-node markers use a circular blue badge with a lightning bolt.
   In the current update, only bolt badges grow on hover or keyboard focus.

4. Multi-school map previews
   The user asked for other schools to receive campus-map treatment comparable
   to U-M, including UPenn, UChicago, UIUC, MSU, Northwestern, and additional
   schools. The preview catalog grew to U-M plus 30 non-U-M schools, including
   nearby and geographically distributed institutions. The user also asked
   for roughly 20 schools geographically near U-M and additional highly ranked
   schools near the region. School markers use school imagery where available,
   with prominent schools receiving larger overview markers and smaller
   schools represented by compact points with a hover/focus reveal.

5. Zoom-dependent school markers and campus detail
   Prominent school markers are larger at a far-out overview. At regional
   zoom, smaller markers and school-specific logos/tooltips are used; at
   detailed-campus zoom, those overview markers yield to local campus
   landmarks. This implements the requested transition where large logos
   disappear as the view gets closer and local campus points take over.
   Non-U-M map previews also have a compact information card with campus name,
   logo, landmark count, illustrative-route count, and a preview-only note.

6. Routes, buses, and map navigation
   The user reported that buses/routes disappeared during map movement, that
   buses moved with the map instead of staying geographically anchored, and
   that bus locations changed when a building broke. The route and marker
   overlays now use geographic coordinates and are reprojected during map
   navigation. Bus animation uses stable route identity rather than geometry
   that can split around an outage, and animation progress is paused during
   user navigation. Regional preview routes and power links are explicitly
   labeled illustrative and not verified live transit or infrastructure.

7. Day/night map presentation
   The current update adds a "Night map" switch to the map-layer panel. On the
   vector basemap it recolors eligible background, land, water, road, and label
   layers and adjusts map lighting; 3D building extrusions receive a night
   palette while retaining their height detail. Returning to day mode restores
   the vector style's original static paint colors. On the raster basemap, the
   map canvas receives day/night filters. The underlying detailed vector style,
   street data, building footprints, and 3D building layer are retained.

Important accuracy notes
------------------------
- Only the U-M campus simulation is connected to the existing simulation data.
- Non-U-M campuses, routes, shuttle movement, and energy links are visual
  previews; they must not be presented as verified official/live information.
- The basemap depends on externally hosted map tiles/styles being available.
- The project originally encountered a missing local `spacetime` executable;
  this history file does not claim that the SpacetimeDB CLI was installed.
- Historical requests are summarized from available conversation context;
  this document intentionally does not reproduce messages verbatim.
