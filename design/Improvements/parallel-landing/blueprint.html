What do I do with it?

Break it. Watch it cascade. Branch. Adopt.

01 · Break something

Trip a feed or start a heat wave

Fail the Central Power Plant, or cut it to 35% and the north feed to 50%. Or click any building and fail it.

02 · Watch it cascade

Twenty buildings on three feeds react

An energy agent sheds load. A transit agent moves people out of dark buildings. Nothing is scripted. No agent can invent power that is not there.

03 · Branch the timeline

Four policies on four copies

The engine forks the campus and runs each response policy forward on its own copy, with the same agents and the same supply.

04 · Compare and adopt

Pick one. The live campus switches to it.

Essential demand served, people relocated, buildings dark, side by side. Adopt a timeline and the live campus follows it.

Can I trust the numbers?

Real campus. Hard constraints. Not a forecast.

This is a constraint-based teaching model, not a forecast of the real grid. The numbers come from running the simulation. Use them to practise a decision, not to predict an outage.

  • 20Real buildings. Markley, South Quad, University Hospital, Mott, Duderstadt, NCRC and fourteen more, with demand in kW and occupancy.
  • 3Real feeds. Central Power Plant, the medical campus tie, and the North Campus Switching Station, wired the way the campus is.
  • 38Power lines and roads. Fifteen lines carry power. Twenty-three roads carry people when the transit agent moves them.
  • 0Invented kilowatts. Supply is a hard limit on every feed. Agents shed, reroute and relocate. They never create power.
